# anim-review (review)

Captures the animation (pose snapshots, film strips, onion sheet), computes its numbers, runs three blind reviewers (A poses and silhouette, B timing and spacing, C weight and follow-through; each also scores fidelity and Overall), then records the user's verdict, which wins. At most 2 panel rounds per build.
