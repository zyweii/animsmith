# anim-make (build loop)

Runs one animation build in ANIM LAB from a brief: one question round (the delivery target is always asked), references from the librarian, a beat sheet in `LOG.md`, then passes (blocking, timing and splining, polish, delivery), each ending with numbers (lint, motion, feet, timing) and pose snapshots or film strips; the user checks blocking alone, and the blind panel then the user review after polish and at delivery. `/anim study <ID>` rebuilds one reference at blocking level and scores it with `anim_compare` to check its Breakdown.
